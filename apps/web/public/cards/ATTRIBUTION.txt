Spanish playing card artwork — Heraclio Fournier, 1878 deck

The card faces and the reverse are the individual card images of the first deck
printed by Naipes Heraclio Fournier (Vitoria-Gasteiz, 1878, ref. 44470).

Authors credited on Wikimedia Commons: Ignacio Díaz Olano, Heraclio Fournier González, Emilio Soubrier

        bosteko, seiko, zazpiko, txota, zaldi, errege · bastoia, ezpata, kopa, urrea) and "Atzealdea" (back).

Modifications for use in this app: transparent card edges cropped, resized to 480×760 px,
